Vrajix POS

Billing, stock, purchases and customer accounts for small shops. Works on any phone, tablet or computer, even without internet.

Open the app

Fast billing

Tap products to make a bill, print on a Bluetooth printer or share it on WhatsApp as text or PDF, in English or Gujarati.

Stock and purchases

Stock goes down with every bill and up with every purchase, with boxes and pieces, low-stock alerts and buying cost.

Customer accounts

Each customer's dues, payments and statement in one place, with call and WhatsApp in one tap.

Safe backups

Backups go to the shop's own Google Drive and, with a Vrajix code, to Vrajix Cloud. Restore any time on a new device.

How sign-in and data work

  • The shop signs in with its Google account. One Gmail is one shop.
  • All shop data is kept on the shop's own device. The app works offline after the first sign-in.
  • Google Drive backup uses only the folder and files the app creates. Vrajix POS cannot see any other file in the Drive, or the shop's Gmail.
  • We do not sell data, show ads or use the data for marketing.

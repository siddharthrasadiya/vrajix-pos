Privacy Policy

Vrajix POS · Last updated 12 November 2026

Vrajix POS ("the app") is a billing, stock and customer-account app for shops, made by Vrajix, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what information the app uses, why, where it is kept and the choices you have.

1. Information the app uses

  • Your Google account email address. You sign in with Google. The app reads only your email address, to know which shop is signing in. We do not get your Google password.
  • Shop data you enter: products, prices, stock, purchases, bills, customers (including their names, mobile numbers and addresses that you type in), payments and settings.
  • Device information: a random number made by the app for your device and a short device name such as "Android · Chrome 141". It is used to keep each shop open on one device at a time and to help with support.
  • Records of cloud requests: when the app talks to Vrajix Cloud, we keep the time, your email, your Vrajix code, the action (for example "backup"), its size, whether it worked and the device name.

The app does not use advertising, tracking or analytics services.

2. Google permissions the app asks for

  • See your email address (userinfo.email): to sign you in and to match your shop.
  • Google Drive, files created by this app only (drive.file): to save and read your backup files in a "Vrajix POS Backups" folder. The app cannot see, open or change any other file in your Drive.

Vrajix POS's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements. Google user data is used only to provide the features described here. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not read by people except with your permission, for security, or when the law requires it.

3. Where your information is kept

  • On your device: all shop data is kept in the app's storage on your phone, tablet or computer.
  • In your Google Drive (when you turn on Google Drive backup): backup files in your own Google account, under your control.
  • In Vrajix Cloud (only when you enter a Vrajix code): a copy of your backups in the Vrajix Google Drive, in a folder for your shop only, and your shop's row (shop name, email, code, approval) in the Vrajix Google Sheet. These are stored on Google's servers.

4. How we use it

  • To sign you in, approve your shop and keep it on one device at a time.
  • To save, list and restore your backups.
  • To help you when you ask for support, and to keep the service safe.

We do not sell or rent your information or your customers' information, and we do not use it for marketing. We share it only when the law requires it.

5. How long it is kept

  • On your device: until you remove the shop from the device (Settings → Account).
  • Google Drive and Vrajix Cloud: the newest 30 backups; older ones are deleted.
  • Vrajix Cloud backups of a shop turned off for 90 days may be deleted.
  • Records of cloud requests: 90 days.

6. Your choices

  • Turn off Google Drive or Vrajix Cloud backup at any time in Settings → Backup.
  • Log out, or remove all of a shop's data from a device, in Settings → Account.
  • Remove the app's access to your Google account at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
  • Ask us to delete your Vrajix Cloud backups and your shop's records by email. Backups in your own Google Drive are yours to delete.

7. Your customers' information

You decide which customer details to enter. You are responsible for collecting them with your customers' agreement and only for your business.

8. Security

Connections use HTTPS. Google checks every sign-in, and each shop reaches only its own backups. An owner PIN can protect changes on the device. No method is completely secure, so keep your Google account, Vrajix code and PIN safe.

9. Children

The app is for businesses and is not meant for children under 18.

10. Changes

We will update this page when the policy changes and show the new date at the top.
